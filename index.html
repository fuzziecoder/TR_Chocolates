Our Lineage

A Little Kodai, A Lot of Love.

TR Homemade Chocolates in Hosur celebrates Kodaikanal-style homemade chocolates and selected Kodai special products.

Born from a passion for honest ingredients and hill confectionery heritage, our creations are slow-tempered and prepared in small batches. We take pride in delivering natural flavours and comforting richness directly to chocolate enthusiasts in Hosur and beyond.

Our Principles

Why Choose TR Homemade

Honoring genuine hill craftsmanship, authentic natural flavours, and uncompromising care.

🤎

Homemade with Love

Every piece of chocolate is prepared in micro-batches with meticulous patience and genuine artisanal affection.

🌿

Real Ingredients

Pure cocoa butter, stone-ground cacao, whole nuts, and unadulterated forest honeys. Zero synthetic stabilizers.

⛰️

Kodaikanal-Style

Inspired by the revered confectionery traditions and cool hill climate recipes of southern hill stations.

✨

Thoughtfully Selected

From single-origin bars to wild raw honey and botanical extracts, every creation is hand-chosen for purity.
